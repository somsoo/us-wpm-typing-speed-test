🧮 How the score works

  • Words per minute: typing tests count one word as 5 characters, including spaces and punctuation. WPM = characters that match the text ÷ 5 ÷ minutes. Example: 300 correct characters in 1 minute = 60 WPM; in 30 seconds the same 300 characters would be 120 WPM.
  • Raw WPM: every character you typed ÷ 5 ÷ minutes, right or wrong. The gap between raw WPM and WPM shows how much speed mistakes cost you.
  • Accuracy: correct keystrokes ÷ all keystrokes. A wrong key still counts after you fix it with Backspace. Accuracy is rounded down, so 100% means no wrong keys at all.
  • Uncorrected errors: wrong characters still in place when the test ends.
  • Timing: the clock starts with your first key and stops at the chosen length, or earlier if you type the whole text. WPM and raw WPM are rounded to whole numbers.

❓ Frequently asked questions

How is WPM calculated?

Typing tests count one word as five characters, including spaces and punctuation. This test divides the number of characters you typed correctly by 5, then by the minutes of the test. Raw WPM uses every character you typed, right or wrong. For example, 300 correct characters in 60 seconds is 60 WPM.

How is accuracy calculated?

Accuracy is the share of your keystrokes that matched the text when you typed them. A mistake still counts against accuracy after you fix it with Backspace, because it was a wrong keystroke. Uncorrected errors are the wrong characters still left when the test ends.

Why can't I paste text?

Pasting is turned off during the test so the score shows your own typing. The Enter key does not add a new line either, because the practice text is one long paragraph.

Which test length should I choose?

Short tests reward a quick burst of speed, while longer tests show how well you keep your pace. Take the same length a few times and compare; your best score for each length is kept in this browser.

Is my typing saved or sent anywhere?

No. What you type stays on this page and is never sent to us. Only your best score and its date for each test length are saved in this browser, and you can clear them at any time.
